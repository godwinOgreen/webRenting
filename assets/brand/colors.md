# 0xRenting Color System

---

## Philosophy

The colour palette reflects the three pillars of the platform.

- Property
- Engineering
- Cloud Infrastructure

The palette should feel premium, modern and technical rather than resembling a traditional real estate company.

## Rules

Purple is the brand colour.

Blue represents cloud infrastructure.

Cyan represents technology.

Never overuse gradients.

Dark backgrounds should dominate.
