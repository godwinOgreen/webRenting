# 0xRenting Brand Guidelines

> Engineering Rental Platforms the Right Way.

---

## Brand Vision

0xRenting is an open-source engineering platform focused on demonstrating how modern rental marketplaces should be architected, engineered and deployed.

The visual identity should communicate:

- Trust
- Professionalism
- Modern Engineering
- Scalability
- Simplicity
- Innovation

The brand should feel equally at home on GitHub, technical documentation, conference presentations and future commercial products.

---

## Brand Personality

The platform should feel:

- Modern
- Technical
- Minimal
- Professional
- Premium
- Approachable

Avoid playful or cartoon-like designs.

Every visual element should reinforce confidence and engineering excellence.

---

## Brand Values

🏛 Architecture First

📖 Documentation Matters

🔒 Secure by Default

⚡ Built to Scale

🧩 Modular by Design

🌍 Open Source

🎯 Engineering Excellence

📚 Continuous Learning

---

## Brand Promise

0xRenting exists to demonstrate how production-grade rental platforms should be engineered.

Every piece of documentation, code and design should reinforce this promise.

---

## Visual Identity

The visual language combines three themes:

• Property
• Software Engineering
• Cloud Infrastructure

Rather than looking like a traditional real estate company, the brand should represent a modern software platform powering property marketplaces.

---

## Logo Concept

The logo should communicate the core purpose of the platform at a glance.

The preferred concept consists of:

- A modern house outline
- Two simple people standing in front of the house
- The "0x" integrated into the house design
- Minimal geometric styling
- Rounded corners
- Clean line art

The logo should remain recognisable even at small sizes.

---

## Illustration Style

Illustrations should use:

- Isometric perspective
- Modern architecture
- Cloud-native themes
- Network connections
- Minimal line icons
- Soft lighting
- Purple and blue accents

Avoid stock photography.

---

## Design Philosophy

The design should feel similar in quality to products such as:

- Vercel
- Stripe
- Supabase
- Linear
- Appwrite

Simple.

Elegant.

Purposeful.

---

## Tagline

Engineering Rental Platforms the Right Way.

This tagline should appear consistently across branding materials whenever appropriate.

---

## Brand Mission

The visual identity should communicate one simple message:

Professional software engineering for modern rental platforms.
